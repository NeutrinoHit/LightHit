# Accuracy and speed

## What the test suite checks

The tests (`pytest tests src/lighthit/tests`) use synthetic media and
detectors. They check exact identities and tolerances of the numerical
methods, for example:

* table building, cache keys, readout, acceptance contraction (`test_cache*`,
  `test_directional*`, `test_readout`, `test_model_file`);
* the shower route against the per-band basis route: orders 0 and 1 equal,
  order >=2 within 2% charge and 5% bin L1 (`test_shower_route`);
* order 1 against independent quadrature and Monte-Carlo references
  (`test_prompt`, `test_segment_reference`);
* event composition and the laser flash (`test_event`);
* muon building blocks (`test_joint_muon`, `test_universal_primary`, ...).

They do not check the Geant4/Baikal-GVD numbers below.

## Current measurements

`experiments/REPORT.md` holds the measured accuracy and speed of the default
routes and of every alternative in the registry (`experiments/ideas/`), with
date, machine, commit and input hashes, on the standard inputs (data bundle and
NTSim events regenerated by `experiments/regenerate_inputs.py`). The section
below is kept as it was reported during development; its inputs and runs were
not preserved, and the registry measurements replace it.

Default routes on the standard inputs (2026-10-17, commit 97372c7, Apple M4 Pro,
1440 OMs in five clusters). Errors are against the references of the registry:
orders 0 and 1 on the original Geant4 steps (`element_method="segment"` for
showers, grouped per-band pencils for muons, no screen, no band fusion), order
>=2 by the per-band cloud-in-cell route (electron, 300 GeV muon) or by the shower
basis refined to 0.125 m, 0.01 ns and band-fold rank 9 (pion, nu_e CC). L1 is the
summed absolute bin difference over the summed reference bins of the OMs active
in both; order 0 agrees exactly on all inputs.

| Input | Route | Wall, 1 thread / all | Order 1 bias, L1 | Order >=2 bias, L1 | Total L1 |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| 100 GeV e (bundle event 5) | shower | 14.4 s / 7.7 s | -0.50%, 1.14% | -0.003%, 0.31% | 0.39% |
| 100 GeV pi+ | shower | 20.7 s / 3.5 s | -0.57%, 10.6% | +0.007%, 0.044% | 3.5% |
| nu_e CC (e 70, pi+ 25, p 5 GeV) | shower | 13.8 s / 2.8 s | -0.52%, 2.3% | -0.014%, 0.058% | 0.78% |
| 1 TeV muon (bundle) | muon | 52.2 s / 33.5 s | +2.32%, 2.50% | no reference (memory) | |
| 300 GeV muon | muon | 60.5 s / 51.8 s | +3.24%, 2.97% | +0.70%, 0.42% | 0.95% |
| 3 TeV muon | muon | 124.6 s / 76.1 s | no reference | | |

Against one NTSim photon Monte Carlo run per event (every photon, 30
scatterings, the regenerated events), the total charge of `EventTransport` is
1.054 (100 GeV e, MC 5.59 +- 0.22 pe), 1.009 (pi+), 0.928 (nu_e CC) and 1.110
(100 GeV muon, computed by the shower fallback) times the MC charge. The
regenerated 100 GeV muon fails in `MuonTransport` (`known-issues.md`).

## Reported during development (LightHit 0.3.0a1, not reproduced)

NTSim/Geant4 events in five Baikal-GVD clusters (1440 OMs), one CPU thread,
after Numba compilation. Errors are relative to calculations on the original
Geant4 steps or to a finer source basis, not to photon Monte Carlo.

| Event | Time |
|---|---:|
| electron 100 GeV | 5.5-6.4 s |
| electron 1 TeV | 42 s |
| pi+ 100 GeV | 21 s |
| nu_e CC (e 70 + pi 25 + p 5 GeV) | 14 s |
| muon 100 GeV / 1 TeV | 11 / 10 s |
| nu_mu CC (mu 800 + pi 150 + p 50 GeV) | 73 s |

* Order 0 is exact on the Geant4 steps over the continuous spectrum. A
  nine-wavelength quadrature underestimated it by about 9% for showers (up to
  100% on single OMs).
* Order 1 (grouped, fused bands) against original-step integration: bin L1 at
  most 3.3% (100 GeV) and 3.1% (1 TeV), charge bias below 0.6%. This is the
  largest numerical error of the shower calculation.
* Order >=2 against a finer basis: at most 0.15% bin L1 on 100 GeV electrons,
  0.4% at 1 TeV, 1% on a hadronic shower.
* Hadronic showers emit about 3% of their light 50 ns to milliseconds late;
  that light is read on its own shifted clock or counted in the charge only.

## Reproducible comparison with photon Monte Carlo

`examples/LightHit/lighthit-in-ntsim.ipynb` in NTSim (section 2) runs one
Geant4 event (100 GeV electron) through `LightHitTransport` and through
NTSim's `CherenkovGenerator` -> `MCPhotonPropagator` (up to 30 scatterings,
full photon statistics) -> `SmartRayTracer`. LightHit gives 5.89 pe in about
6 s; three Monte-Carlo runs gave 5.82, 5.86 and 5.48 pe (each +- 0.23
statistical) in about 320 s. Per-OM charges and arrival times agree within
about two Monte-Carlo standard deviations.
