# Superseded material

These files are the previous edition of the book and are **not** part of it:
they are not listed in `_quarto.yml` and are not rendered. They are kept only
as source material while the chapters of the current structure are written, and
are deleted chapter by chapter as the material they hold is either carried over
or explicitly rejected in the appendix of rejected methods.

Nothing here should be cited from the book.
